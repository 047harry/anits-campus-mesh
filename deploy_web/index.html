Multi-Device Testing Guide

1

Install on 2+ Android Phones

Download the APK on both devices. If prompted, tap "Settings ➔ Allow from this source" to install.

2

Grant Nearby Devices & Bluetooth

Launch the app. Grant the requested Bluetooth & Location permissions so the radios can scan and broadcast.

3

Simulate Total Blackout (Turn Off Wi-Fi & Data)

Turn OFF Mobile Data and Wi-Fi Internet on both phones (leave Bluetooth ON). Bring devices within 30m.

4

Observe Real-Time Mesh Relay

Watch both devices instantly discover each other. Send an offline chat or tap EMERGENCY SOS to witness priority routing across the mesh!
